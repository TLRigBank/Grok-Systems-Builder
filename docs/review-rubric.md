# Portfolio Review Rubric (Draft)

Reviewers evaluate submitted portfolios against the following dimensions.  
Scores and qualitative notes are shared with the learner.

## 1. Technical Competence
- Does the system actually run?
- Are tools, agents, and grounding used appropriately?
- Is error handling present and sensible?

## 2. Architectural Judgment
- Are the major design choices explained and justified?
- Does the author show awareness of alternatives and trade-offs?
- Is complexity justified by the problem?

## 3. Clarity & Documentation
- Can another practitioner understand and run the work?
- Is the narrative honest about limitations?
- Are evaluation results presented clearly?

## 4. Learning Signal
- Does the portfolio demonstrate growth across the path?
- Are earlier artifacts visibly improved or integrated?
- Is there evidence of reflection?

## 5. Overall Signal
- Would you trust this person to design or lead a real Grok-based system under constraints?

---

**Outcomes**
- **Approved** → Verified credential + optional public gallery
- **Needs Revision** → Specific feedback returned; learner may resubmit
- **Not Ready** → Clear gaps identified; further module work recommended

Rubrics will be published and versioned. Review is never a black box.
