# Competency Domains

These domains guide both content design and portfolio review.

1. **Systems Thinking with Grok**  
   Ability to move beyond single prompts into coherent, maintainable systems.

2. **Tool & Grounding Design**  
   Clean interfaces, reliable external data, graceful failure handling.

3. **Multi-Agent Orchestration**  
   Role design, handoffs, critique/synthesis patterns, state management.

4. **Production Reliability**  
   Evaluation, observability, cost awareness, and hardening under real constraints.

5. **Architectural Judgment**  
   Trade-off analysis, knowing when *not* to add complexity, clear decision records.

6. **Communication & Proof**  
   Documentation, narrative clarity, and the ability to demonstrate work to other practitioners.

A strong portfolio shows evidence across these domains rather than excellence in only one.
